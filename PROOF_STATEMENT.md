# What Are You Proving?

## Proof Statement

My portfolio proves that I can build modern frontend web applications using React, Next.js, and TypeScript. It is designed for a hiring manager recruiting frontend developer interns or junior frontend developers who wants evidence of practical skills through real projects and case studies. My goal is for that hiring manager to invite me for an interview after reviewing my portfolio.

## Why This Exists

My CV and LinkedIn list my skills, but they cannot demonstrate how I solve real frontend problems or the quality of the applications I build. My portfolio provides that proof through real projects and technical decisions.
